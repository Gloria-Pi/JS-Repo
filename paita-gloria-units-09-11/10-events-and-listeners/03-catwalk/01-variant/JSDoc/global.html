catWalkWithReset()

Moves the cat 10 pixels to the right on each call. - If the cat reaches the right edge of the screen (based on the window width and the cat's width), its position resets to the left side (`left = 0px`). - Otherwise, the cat continues moving to the right by 10 pixels. This function creates a continuous looping animation across the screen.
