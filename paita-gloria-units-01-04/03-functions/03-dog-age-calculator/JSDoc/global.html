calculateDogAgeBonus(dogAge, conversionRate) → {void}

This function calculates a dog's age in dog years based on the given conversion rate and logs the result to the console. The function takes the dog's age in human years and multiplies it by the provided conversion rate to determine the equivalent dog years.
