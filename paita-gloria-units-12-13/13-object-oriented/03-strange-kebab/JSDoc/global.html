toStrangeKebab() → {string}

Converts the current string into a "strange kebab-case" format. This transformation: - Detects camelCase and PascalCase boundaries - Replaces non-alphanumeric characters and spaces with hyphens - Collapses multiple hyphens into one - Removes leading digits (replacing them with a hyphen first, then trimming) - Removes hyphens from start/end - Converts the final result to lowercase
